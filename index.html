For Uber & Bolt drivers

Get fingerprinted today. No appointment.

Uber and Bolt need a biometric fingerprint capture before they’ll approve you as a driver-partner. Walk in with your documents and we’ll take you through it — no long queues, no confusion.

R350per capture
  • Walk-in

What to bring

3 items
  • South African ID or passportID book, smart ID card or a valid passport — the original.
  • Proof of residenceUtility bill, bank statement or affidavit, not older than 3 months.
  • Driver’s licence or PrDPOptional — not needed for the capture, but it speeds up registration.Optional

How it works

4 steps
  1. 1Gather documentsUse the list above
  2. 2Walk inR350 capture
  3. 3RegisterOn the Uber or Bolt app
  4. 4Get approvedBy the platform

Final approval and processing times are set by Uber and Bolt, not by Web Graphs Technologies.
