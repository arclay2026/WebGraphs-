Everything you need to get fingerprinted and registered as a driver-partner —

1Documents to bring

South African ID or passport

Your ID book, smart ID card or a valid passport. Bring the original.

Proof of residence

A utility bill, bank statement or affidavit — not older than 3 months.

Driver’s licence / PrDP if you have one

Not required to get fingerprinted, but bring it along if you already have it — it speeds up your platform registration.

3Quick steps

  1. Gather your documents using the checklist above.
  2. Visit us for your fingerprint capture — R350, walk-in.
  3. Register on the Uber or Bolt app and upload your documents when asked.
  4. Wait for approval while Uber or Bolt reviews your account.

Final approval and processing times are set by Uber and Bolt directly, not by Web Graphs Technologies.
