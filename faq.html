Fingerprint capture

How much does fingerprint capture cost?

R350 per capture for Uber and Bolt driver-partner registration.

What documents do I need to bring?

Your South African ID or passport, and proof of residence not older than 3 months. If you already have a driver’s licence or PrDP, bring that too — it speeds up your registration. Download the checklist (PDF).

Do I need to book an appointment?

No — walk in with your documents and we’ll guide you through the whole process, no long queues.
